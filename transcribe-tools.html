Transcribe tools

Live transcribe apps, compared

Not everyone around you uses the same phone. Compare free and paid tools for iOS and Android, so you know which one to recommend to friends and classmates.

Overview

Live Transcribe

Android · Free, by Google

  • Real-time captions on your phone
  • Sound alerts, for example a doorbell
  • Saves transcripts for a few days

Live Captions

iPhone, iPad and Mac · Built in, free

  • Turn it on in Accessibility settings
  • Works across apps and calls
  • No extra app needed

Ava

iOS & Android · Free plan, paid plan from about €9 a month (£8)

  • Works on both phone types
  • Made for group conversations
  • Save and share transcripts
